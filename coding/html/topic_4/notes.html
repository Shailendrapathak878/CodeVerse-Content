Heading Hierarchy (h1 to h6)

HTML provides six levels of headings from <h1> to <h6>. <h1> represents the most important heading on the page, and <h6> the least.

Search engines and screen readers rely on heading hierarchy to index page structure. It is a critical accessibility best practice to only have one <h1> per page representing the overall topic.

Line Breaks (
) and Thematic Breaks (

The <br> tag produces a single line break without starting a new paragraph. It is ideal for poems, physical street addresses, or song lyrics.

The <hr> tag represents a thematic break or transition between topics, typically rendered as a horizontal divider line.
